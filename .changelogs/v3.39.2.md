# Release v3.39.2

Released: 2026-09-21

## Highlights

- **OpenCode review admission now fails closed.** Reviews that cannot access their provider or model are reported as inconclusive instead of being mistaken for clean results, protecting merge decisions.
- **OpenCode reviewer diagnostics are actionable without exposing provider output.** Operators receive a safe explanation and remedy while raw responses and sensitive details stay out of user-facing diagnostics.
- **Swarm reviewer preflight recognizes provider admission failures.** Required OpenCode reviewers are rejected before claims are partitioned or work is assigned.

## Fixed

- Preserve OpenCode provider-admission denial as `no-verdict` so reviewer aggregation can continue safely without treating the run as clean.
- Treat synthetic `HTTP 403 / FreeTierError` responses as unavailable during swarm reviewer preflight and recommend selecting a supported reviewer, model, or provider (#267).
- Keep optional OpenCode admission failures visible while excluding their inconclusive status from the aggregate merge gate.
